Sprout – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026

Sprout is a casual game for all ages made by Tinsley Studio (Alexander Tinsley) ("we"). We built Sprout so that it does not need to know anything about you. This policy explains exactly what that means.

The short version

Sprout does not collect any personal information itself, there are no accounts, and adverts shown to children are never personalised. The app shows optional reward adverts (you choose to watch one in exchange for in-game currency) and offers optional in-app purchases, both handled by Google. Those two features are the only way any data leaves your device, and they're described below.

Your game progress

Your progress (dew, flowers, missions, settings and so on) is saved only on your own device. We never receive it. If you have Android's own device backup switched on, Android may include this game data in your personal Google backup, which is managed by Google under your Google account settings, not by us. Uninstalling Sprout, or using "Reset all progress" in the game's Settings, deletes it from your device.

Permissions the app uses

  • Vibration – for small buzzes when you play. You can switch this off in Settings.
  • Internet and view network connections – needed to show adverts, process purchases, and to check whether your phone is online so those features aren't offered while you're offline.

Adverts

Sprout shows optional reward adverts through Google AdMob: you choose to watch one, in exchange for in-game dew. No advert plays without you choosing to watch it, and there are no banner or pop-up adverts anywhere in the app.

Your age and adverts

The first time you open Sprout, it asks for your year of birth. This answer is stored only on your device and is never sent to us or to Google. Sprout uses it for one thing: to decide which kind of adverts are allowed.

  • Players aged 13 or under: every advert request is marked as child-directed under Google's Families policy. Adverts are never personalised, and your device's advertising ID is not used.
  • Players aged 14 and over: Google's advertising services may collect your device's advertising ID and other technical information, and may use it to personalise adverts. In the UK, EU and other places where the law requires it, Sprout asks for your consent before this happens. Players aged 16 or under are also treated as below the age of digital consent in those places.

Whatever your age, adverts are limited to content suitable for everyone. Google AdMob may also collect limited technical information, such as device type, operating system, approximate location from your IP address and how you interact with adverts, so that it can deliver and measure adverts and prevent fraud. You can change your ad choices at any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices, where available. Google's own privacy policy explains what it collects: policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Further detail on how AdMob handles this data is at support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

In-app purchases

Sprout's shop (extra dew and flower packs) is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Payment details are given to Google, not to us; we only learn that a purchase for a particular item was completed, so we can unlock it in the game. See Google Play's own privacy policy for how it handles payments: policies.google.com/privacy.

Sharing pictures

When you tap "Share", Sprout creates a picture of your plant and hands it to the app you choose (for example a messaging app). We never see or receive the picture. What happens next is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

Children

Sprout is suitable for everyone, including children, and participates in Google Play's Families programme. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Sprout asks every player for their year of birth when they first open it. Players aged 13 or under only ever get child-directed, non-personalised adverts from Google AdMob, which is self-certified for use in Google Play's Families programme. In-app purchases are protected by Google Play's own purchase controls, including parental approval where a family has set it up. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question about your child's data, please contact us using the email below.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Sprout changes how data is handled, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the effective date at the top of this page.
